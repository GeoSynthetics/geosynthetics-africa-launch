Overview

BERA Gravel Fix® Pro is an advanced, recyclable stabilisation system designed for gravel, shells, mulch and sand. Featuring a high-quality polypropylene honeycomb structure on a thermally-welded DuPont™ Typar® geotextile base, it provides a strong, stable sub-base for professional gravel applications in paths, driveways, car parks and roof terraces.

Geosynthetics Africa is the appointed BERA® distribution and installation partner for the African continent. We hold continuous stock across the region, provide pre-tender BoQ assistance, engineering review, on-site contractor support and complete certification documentation. The system is EU-manufactured under ISO 9001 and EPD certification, REACH-compliant, and proven across 47+ countries on five continents.

Why specifiers choose Gravel Fix Pro

Sustainably manufactured

100% recycled raw materials at no extra cost. No microplastics. No glue — assembled with thermally-welded connections. Produced in Europe using solar and biomass energy.

Exceptional performance

Rainwater infiltration up to 180 l/(m²·s). Vertical strength >450 t/m² when filled — vehicle and truck rated. Proven in 47+ countries on five continents.

Versatile & user-friendly

Ideal for parking, driveways, pathways and terraces. Stable under furniture, ladders, high heels, bicycles, motorbikes and wheelchairs. No specialist machinery required.

Doc 2 §5 → 8-step procedure

Installation

Summary of the manufacturer's installation sequence. The complete step-by-step procedure, foundation schedule by load case, tools list and common-mistakes checklist is in the Installation Guide below.

1

Foundation

15–25 cm compacted MOT for vehicles; 10 cm sharp sand for paths. Compact to ≥95 % Proctor.

2

Shaping

Set the perimeter with BERA® Edge — aluminium, Corten, galvanised steel or polymer.

3

Compacting

Plate compactor in 15 cm lifts. Compaction is the single biggest factor in long-term performance.

4

Levelling

Screed 4–6 cm bedding (vehicles) / 10 cm (paths). Remove debris and sharp items.

5

Laying

Stretcher-bond. Engage the extended Typar® lip of each sheet onto the adjacent sheet.

6

Trimming

Cut to fit edges and obstacles with handsaw, jigsaw or circular saw.

7

Filling

Fill cells with 3–16 mm aggregate; top-dress 15–20 mm above cell wall height.

8

Finishing

Rake evenly. Maintenance = raking and removing debris. Top up gravel as required.
