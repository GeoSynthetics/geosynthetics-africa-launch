PROJECT-SPECIFIC · the brief

In Q3 2024, the environmental affairs team of an operational gold mine in West Africa engaged Geosynthetics Africa to conduct an independent integrity review of the HDPE composite lining installed on their primary TSF six years earlier — ahead of a scheduled regulatory audit and as part of the operator's voluntary alignment with the Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management (GISTM).

The lining had been installed by a third-party contractor in 2018. No major operational concerns had been flagged, but the operator wanted independent verification that the system was performing as designed before submitting their next Tailings Operating Plan to the regulator.

The scope was structured as a pure services engagement — no materials supply, no installation. GSA was selected on technical credentials only.

CONVERT · independence statement is the trust signal
01Independence

Independent. By design.

For a review to carry weight with a regulator or an Independent Tailings Review Board (ITRB), the reviewer must have no commercial stake in the outcome. The original installer cannot review their own work; the material supplier cannot review the material they sold. Independence is the entire point of a third-party integrity review.

Our independence statement on this engagement

For the avoidance of doubt and on the formal record of the engagement:

  • GSA did not supply the original lining material installed in 2018
  • GSA did not install the original lining or any subsequent repairs
  • GSA has no commercial relationship with the original installation contractor or the original material supplier
  • GSA's engagement is fee-only — billed on time and materials, with no contingent fee, no success fee, and no kickback structure
  • The review team consists of IAGI-certified inspectors and ISO 17025–aligned laboratory staff who report findings without commercial influence
  • The report goes to the operator and, with the operator's written authorisation, to the regulator unchanged
PROJECT-SPECIFIC · the methodology — defines the report's credibility
02Methodology

A five-stage forensic protocol.

The review followed the standard ITRB-aligned five-stage liner integrity protocol — adapted with the operator's environmental affairs team for site-specific access constraints and tailings depositional schedule.

01

Document review

Original CQA pack, installation drawings, as-built records, repair logs, monitoring data from 2018 onwards. Identifies the baseline against which current condition is measured.

OutputBaseline summary & sampling plan
02

Site walk-over & visual inspection

Two-day site visit by senior IAGI inspector. Walks the exposed liner perimeter, anchor trenches, instrumentation pits, leak-detection sumps. Photographs all defects, records GPS.

OutputVisual inspection register, GPS map
03

Sample extraction

94 coupons cut from exposed liner using cold-cut technique (no heat-affected zone). Strategic locations: anchor trench, slope crests, repair patches, penetration boots. Samples sealed, batched, shipped to laboratory under chain-of-custody.

OutputSample register, chain-of-custody log
04

Laboratory testing

Independent ISO 17025–aligned laboratory testing. Six test streams: thickness, density, tensile, OIT (standard & high pressure), ESCR (SP-NCTL), carbon black content/dispersion.

OutputLab test certificates per sample
05

Electrical leak location

Where the liner is uncovered (or covered with conductive water), full-footprint electrical leak survey using the water lance / spark survey technique. Locates discrete leaks down to pinhole scale.

OutputLeak location map, repair register
PROJECT-SPECIFIC · the test programme
03Test Programme

Six test streams.
94 samples. One independent lab.

The test matrix was scoped around the failure modes most relevant to a 6-year-in-service HDPE liner exposed to tailings chemistry and UV: oxidative degradation (OIT), stress-induced cracking (ESCR), thickness loss, and tensile property retention.

ASTM D5199 · ASTM D5994

Thickness Verification

Sample-by-sample thickness measurement at multiple points. Compared to original specification thickness and to mill-cert baseline values from 2018 installation. Detects gradual loss or localised damage.

ASTM D5199ASTM D5994
94 samples · 470 measurements
ASTM D6693

Tensile Properties

Yield strength, break strength, elongation at yield, elongation at break. The primary indicator of polymer chain integrity. Loss of tensile properties signals oxidative ageing or chemical attack.

ASTM D6693ISO 527
48 samples · 240 specimens
ASTM D3895 · D5885

OIT — Oxidative Induction Time

Standard OIT (D3895) and High-Pressure OIT (D5885). Direct measurement of remaining antioxidant. The single most predictive test for long-term HDPE durability — and the test most likely to reveal a problem.

ASTM D3895ASTM D5885
94 samples · standard + HP
ASTM D5397

ESCR (SP-NCTL)

Single-Point Notched Constant Tensile Load — environmental stress cracking resistance. Predicts long-term performance under combined load and chemical environment. Critical for slope-installed liner.

ASTM D5397
24 samples · slope locations
ASTM D1505 · D4218 · D5596

Density, Carbon Black, Dispersion

Confirms material identity (still HDPE, not crystallised), confirms carbon black content for UV protection, confirms carbon black dispersion (poor dispersion = stress concentrators).

D1505D4218D5596
48 samples
ASTM D7007

Electrical Leak Location

Full-footprint survey of exposed and water-covered liner sections. Detects holes down to ~1 mm. Critical baseline for the repair register.

ASTM D7007
68,000 m² surveyed
PROJECT-SPECIFIC · findings (kept generic for confidentiality)
04Findings Summary

What we found, summarised.

The full 142-page report contains the detailed findings. The summary below shows the top-level status of each assessment area — colour-coded for the operator's executive review. Specific values, exact locations and remedial-action specifications are confidential to the operator and not reproduced here.

Pass
Area 01

Material identity & baseline

Density, thickness, carbon black content and dispersion all confirm original HDPE specification. Material is what was installed.

Pass
Area 02

Tensile property retention

Yield and break strength retain >90% of original specification across all samples. No measurable polymer degradation.

Monitor
Area 03

OIT depletion on south-facing slope

Standard OIT has dropped from 148 min (baseline) to 84 min (current) on samples taken from the south-facing exposed slope. Still well above the minimum operational threshold but worth retesting in 3 years.

Pass
Area 04

ESCR retention

Stress crack resistance well above specification. No samples failed below the 500-hour minimum. Median 920 hours.

Action
Area 05

Leak location — 2 discrete pinholes

Electrical leak survey identified two discrete pinhole defects: one at a poorly-detailed penetration boot, one at a previously-repaired patch. Both flagged for repair before next deposition cycle.

Pass
Area 06

Anchor trench & perimeter

Anchor trenches intact, no displacement, no exposure of buried liner edge. No undermining observed in any of the 14 inspected sections.
