01 — Overview

The Liner Specification of Record in African Mining and Water Containment

The GSE® HD series is the high-density polyethylene geomembrane African consulting engineers have specified on tailings storage facilities, heap leach pads, potable water reservoirs and landfill systems for more than three decades. Manufactured from virgin HDPE resin with 2–3% carbon black and a calendered flat-die extrusion process, it conforms to GRI-GM13, SANS 1526, ASQUAL, KIWA, BAM, DIBT and OENORM — the seven specifications most commonly written into African project documents.

Geosynthetics Africa carries the full GSE® HD range across four surface variants — smooth, single-textured, double-textured and conductive — in standard roll widths of 7.0m, 7.5m and 8.0m. Material is supplied with mill test certification per roll. Installation is delivered by IAGI-certified field crews with daily trial-weld logging, full non-destructive seam testing per ASTM D7177 and D5641, and a signed QA dossier at handover.

This category page covers product selection, specifications, applications, system pairings, installation, QA/QC and the SKUs available through the catalogue. For project-specific design support, engage our technical team.

02 — Variants

Four Surface Variants. One Engineering Logic.

Surface texture is specified by interface friction requirement — not by preference. Below: the four GSE® HD variants we stock, with their typical application envelope.

GSE HD® Smooth

0.5 — 3.0 mm

Standard flat finish, lowest interface friction. Specified for flat-base containment — process ponds, raw water reservoirs, tank linings, secondary containment bunds, fire-water dams — where slope stability is not governed by liner friction.

View SKUs

GSE HDt® Single-textured

1.0 — 3.0 mm

Textured one face, smooth on the other. Specified where one interface — typically the soil or fill side — needs friction and the other doesn't. Common on heap leach pad sidewalls and TSF embankment liners.

View SKUs

GSE HDt® Double-textured

1.0 — 3.0 mm

Textured both faces. Highest interface friction. Specified for steep slopes (typically >3H:1V) and for composite liner systems where both interfaces govern stability. Standard on double-liner TSFs and Class A landfills.

View SKUs

GSE HD® Conductive

1.5 — 2.5 mm

Co-extruded with a conductive lower stratum. Enables 100% post-installation spark testing of the deployed liner — locating pinholes and damage that pressurised seam tests cannot detect. Specified where leak verification beyond seams is contractually required.

View SKUs

06 — Selection Guide

Three Questions That Determine the Spec

The geomembrane decision belongs to the design calculation, not the procurement document. These three questions frame the conversation with our engineering team.

1

What is the slope geometry?

Flat base, slopes 4H:1V or shallower → smooth. Moderate slope 3H:1V to 2H:1V → single-textured on the slope face. Steep slope under 2H:1V, or composite system where both interfaces govern stability → double-textured.

2

What head pressure and design life?

Reservoirs under 5 m head, 20-year life → 1.0–1.5 mm. Mining containment, 50+ year life → 2.0 mm baseline, stepping to 2.5 mm for primary TSF liners. Hazardous waste under SANS 10221 → 2.0 mm minimum.

3

Is post-installation leak survey required?

If the project spec calls for 100% leak verification beyond seams → conductive HDPE with spark testing. If electrical leak location survey on standard liner is acceptable → standard HDPE with our electrical leak location service.

07 — Installation

Specification is Half the System. Installation is the Other Half.

Every GSE® HD geomembrane on this page can be installed by Geosynthetics Africa's IAGI-certified field crews. Wedge welding on primary seams; extrusion welding for repairs, T-joints and detailing. Daily trial welds are logged at the start of every shift and at four-hour intervals throughout the working day, with destructive coupons cut and field-tested per ASTM D6392 before primary welds begin.

Every non-destructive seam test is recorded against panel layout. Every panel is signed off before the next is welded. Every project closes with a complete QA dossier — material certificates, welder logs, NDT records, destructive test reports, and an as-built panel drawing — handed over under our signature, not a subcontractor's.

For projects where the client appoints a separate installer, we supply material with mill test certificates and provide installation supervision on request.

08 — Quality Assurance

No System Leaves Site Unverified.

  • Material compliance — mill test certificate per roll, GRI-GM13 conformance verified on arrival.
  • Seam integrity — air-pressure testing on dual-track wedge welds (ASTM D7177); vacuum-box testing on extrusion welds (ASTM D5641).
  • Destructive testing — seam coupons cut at specified intervals, shear and peel tested per ASTM D6392.
  • Electrical leak location — ASTM D7007 (water puddle) on standard liner; ASTM D7240 (arc-test / spark) on conductive liner.
  • Documentation — full QA dossier, as-built panel layout, signed handover under our name.

11 — FAQ

Engineer Questions, Answered

What thickness HDPE should I specify for a tailings storage facility?
For most African TSF applications, 2.0 mm is the baseline specification. Primary liner in double-liner systems often steps up to 2.5 mm. Final thickness depends on tailings chemistry, hydraulic head, slope geometry and design life — engage our technical team for site-specific recommendation.
When should I specify textured rather than smooth?
When slope stability is governed by liner-interface friction. On flat bases of 4H:1V or shallower, smooth is appropriate and lower cost. On slopes steeper than 3H:1V, textured surfaces are typically required to achieve target friction angles in the interface shear analysis. The decision belongs to the slope stability calculation, not the procurement document.
Is GSE® HD suitable for potable water reservoirs?
Yes, where supplied to a potable-water-certified specification. Confirm the specific roll batch carries the required certification with our technical team before installation. Documentation is supplied per consignment.
When should I specify conductive liner?
When the project specification or regulator requires 100% leak verification beyond seam testing. Conductive HDPE enables spark testing of the entire deployed liner before backfill, locating pinholes and damage that pressurised seam tests cannot detect. Standard practice on potable water, hazardous waste, and high-consequence tailings.
What is the design life of HDPE in containment?
Buried HDPE with adequate cover and protection layers has a design life well in excess of 100 years per established antioxidant-depletion modelling. Exposed HDPE in temperate climates is typically rated for 25–30 years before stress-cracking risk increases materially. Application context — UV exposure, temperature cycling, chemistry — drives the final design-life calculation.
Do you supply mill test certificates?
Yes. Every roll supplied through Geosynthetics Africa ships with a mill test certificate from the manufacturer, verifying conformance to GRI-GM13 and any project-specific specification. Certificates are filed against the project QA dossier and handed over at project close.
