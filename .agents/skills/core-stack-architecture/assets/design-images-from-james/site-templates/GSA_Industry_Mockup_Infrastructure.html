04Our Infrastructure Delivery Model
Tender to handover certificate.

Infrastructure programmes run on FIDIC contracts, MDB conditions and road agency frameworks. We work as a Sub-contractor or Direct Supplier under all three. The same five service stages, scaled and documented for civil works programmes.

Stage 01

Design

Reinforced soil design (BS 8006, AASHTO LRFD), pavement structure with geosynthetic credit (TRH 4 / AASHTO 1993), drainage layout.

→ Design submission
Stage 02

Supply

Mill certificates, batch numbering, just-in-time delivery to chainage, inspection at port of entry, customs clearance handled.

→ Material register
Stage 03

Install

Trained installation teams, supervisor training for contractor's own labour, method statements aligned to road agency specifications.

→ As-built record
Stage 04

Test

On-site CBR uplift testing, pull-out tests, sampling for laboratory verification, road agency witness testing.

→ Test certificates
Stage 05

Certify

Materials traceability matrix, installation certification, contribution to engineer's completion certificate, warranty registration.

→ Project pack

Our Infrastructure pack

Every project ships with documentation that the resident engineer, supervising consultant and road agency need to sign off the geosynthetic component of the works.

  • Geosynthetic design report (signed)
  • Mill certificates per delivery
  • Method statement (per application)
  • Inspection & Test Plan (ITP)
  • Daily installation diaries
  • Pull-out / CBR test results
  • As-built drawings
  • Material traceability matrix
  • Warranty registration
  • Operation & Maintenance manual
