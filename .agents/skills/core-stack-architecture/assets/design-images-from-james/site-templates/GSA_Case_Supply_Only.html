PROJECT-SPECIFIC · lede

In Q1 2023, a major international copper producer commissioned Geosynthetics Africa for the supply portion of their Stage 1 TSF lining works at a remote operation in the DRC's Lualaba copper belt. Installation was already contracted to a specialist installer pre-positioned on site. What the client needed — and what international suppliers had repeatedly failed to deliver — was 340 tonnes of certified HDPE liner, delivered to a remote inland site, on programme, with full chain-of-custody documentation regulators would accept.

340 t HDPE loaded for road freight · Durban → Kolwezi · 14 days transit Container packing under GSA supervision, with batch numbers cross-referenced to mill certificates before loading.
PROJECT-SPECIFIC · the framing — why this is hard
01The Logistics Challenge

Mill to site — 4 borders, 14 days, zero tolerance for paperwork errors.

Kolwezi is approximately 3,420 km by road from the port of Durban. Material crosses four borders — South Africa, Botswana, Zambia and into the DRC — through four customs regimes, four sets of documentation requirements, and four windows where a mismatched serial number or missing form can hold a 28-tonne truck for a week.

The client's previous supplier had two consignments held at the Kasumbalesa border for 19 days combined. Programme slippage on their Stage 1 lining works had run to 11 weeks, and the operation's CFO was directly involved in the supplier change.

The brief was specific:

  • 340 tonnes of GSE HDPE 1.5 mm liner, mill-certified to the client's specification clause-by-clause
  • Full SADC and COMESA documentation pack, prepared correctly first time
  • Just-in-time delivery to the welder's programme — no stockpiling on site, no early arrivals that incur demurrage
  • Daily transparency on consignment location, customs status, and projected delivery windows

04Document Chain

Every roll, traceable. Every document, audit-ready.

The full chain-of-custody documentation pack accompanies the consignment from mill to site and is delivered in three forms: hard-copy folder with shipment, digital pack uploaded to the client's project portal, and master copy retained in our archive for the operating life of the facility.

01
Mill Test Certificates (per roll)

Manufacturer's mill certs issued per extrusion batch — 70 unique certs covering 70 rolls, with batch numbers stencilled on roll cores.

SADC Certificate of Origin (Form CO)

Confirms preferential origin under SADC trade protocol — required for customs clearance into the DRC at preferential duty rate.

Bill of Lading (Maersk · sealed containers)

Six 40' containers, sealed at Rotterdam, container numbers and seal numbers logged. No seal breakage permitted en route.

Issued

Pre-shipment Inspection Report (PSI)

Independent third-party (SGS) inspection at Durban — visual condition, batch verification, packing list cross-check.

Issued

Independent test certificates (GSA Johannesburg lab)

Batch sampling of mill rolls, tested for thickness, tensile, OIT and density against client specification. Results filed by batch.
